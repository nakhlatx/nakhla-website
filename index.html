Who we are

A youth-led network for Arab and Muslim students in Texas

Nakhla helps Arab and Muslim students in Texas build connections, skills and futures.

Why “Nakhla”?

نخلة (nakhla) means palm tree in Arabic. The date palm grows across Iraq and the Arab world. It grows slowly, sends its roots deep and feeds people for generations.

That's what we want for every student we work with. Like the palm tree we're named for, Nakhla helps Arab and Muslim youth in Texas grow deep roots in their identity and reach high in their careers.

Nakhla was started by a Texas teen who moved here from Iraq and finished high school early. It's run by young people, for young people.
